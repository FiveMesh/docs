Open your account menu and choose **Profile** to open your personal account page. These settings belong to your signed-in user, even when you are working in an organization.

## Account tabs

| Tab | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Edit your profile and review active browser sessions. |
| **Agent** | Control read access and separately allow confirmed service executions. |
| **Connections** | Link or unlink a supported external account, including Discord. |
| **API keys** | Create and manage Developer API Keys. |
| **Notifications** | Choose notification preferences. |
| **Danger Zone** | Open password reset or request account deletion. |

Server registrations are in **Servers** in the main sidebar. Server API Keys are managed from the selected server's overview, rather than the account API keys tab.

## Review sessions

Under **Profile**, inspect the active sessions attached to your account. Revoke a session you no longer use or do not recognize. Revoking your current session signs you out.

Keep your linked accounts and sign-in methods up to date. If you need a password reset, use the reset link in **Danger Zone** and follow the instructions sent to your email.

## Delete your account

Read the deletion confirmation in **Danger Zone** before proceeding. Account deletion ends access immediately and affects the services and credentials owned by the account. It is different from leaving or deleting an organization.

Export required data and review your subscription and service configuration before confirming. Contact support if you are uncertain about the resources attached to your account.
