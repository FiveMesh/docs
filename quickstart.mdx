This guide takes you from a new account to your first configured service.

## Prerequisites

- A FiveMesh account with a verified email address.
- Trial or subscription access before activating services.
- Your cfx.re join code for Cache, Voice, Logs or Portal.
- Access to `server.cfg` for server-side integrations.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the [FiveMesh dashboard](https://app.fivemesh.io) and sign in. You land on **Overview**.

    You can use the dashboard without an organization or subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate a plan">
    Open **Billing** and choose a plan or confirm your existing subscription. Active and trialing subscriptions allow service setup. Logs requires Growth or Pro.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register a server">
    Open **Servers**, register your cfx.re join code and review the detected details.

    CDN can operate without a registered server. Cache, Voice, Logs and Portal require one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the context">
    Use the selector at the top of the sidebar to choose **My account** or an organization, then select your registered server.

    An organization is optional. It shares access to its owner's resources without moving billing or ownership.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable a service">
    Open **CDN** to configure public asset delivery, **Cache** to proxy resource downloads, **Voice** to create managed PMA-Voice infrastructure, or **Logs** to connect server events.

    Follow the service setup guide below and wait for provisioning to finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>
