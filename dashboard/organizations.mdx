Organizations are optional collaboration spaces. Members work on the owner's shared services, while subscriptions, quotas and resources remain owned by that customer account.

## Create an organization

1. Open **Organization** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Create organization** and enter the name.
3. Complete creation, then select the organization in the sidebar selector.

Creation requires an eligible active plan. Your account can own one active customer organization. You can also join organizations through invitations.

## Invite members

Open **Organization** > **Members**. Enter the member's email, select a role and send the invitation. The dashboard shows pending invitations and an invitation URL you can share.

The recipient signs in with the invited email and reviews the invitation before accepting or declining. Joining changes collaboration access; their own subscription and resources remain on their account.

Owner access cannot be assigned through the invitation form.

## Roles

| Role | Main permissions |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full service access; manages organization settings, invitations, roles and server grants; can delete the organization. |
| **Admin** | Full service access; edits organization settings and invitations. Owner-only role changes, server grants and organization deletion remain restricted. |
| **Operator** | Manages servers and API keys; uploads, deletes and purges CDN assets; creates, purges and preloads Cache; reads, ingests and exports Logs; creates, restarts and deletes Voice. Cannot administer service settings or enable Logs sources. |
| **Developer** | Reads, registers and updates servers; creates and reads API keys; reads and uploads CDN assets; reads and creates Cache; reads and ingests Logs; reads, creates and restarts Voice. Cannot delete resources or enable Logs sources. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only service access, subject to server grants. Cannot export Logs or manage keys, services or organization settings. |

Only the resource owner controls their customer subscription. Organization roles do not transfer billing ownership.

## Server access

Owners can open **Server access** for a member under **Members** and choose **All servers** or **Specific servers**. Selecting no specific servers gives that member no server access.

Server grants restrict read-only roles. Roles that can update servers can reach all servers allowed by their role. A server grant does not add service actions that the role lacks.

## Share services

Organization context gives members access to the owner's shared **CDN**, **Cache**, **Voice** and **Logs**, subject to their role, service availability and server access. The current V2 organization pages are **Overview**, **Members** and **Settings**; invitations are managed under **Members**.

<Note>
  An active owner subscription alone does not grant members service access. The member needs the required role and server access. Ask support to review service availability if those permissions are correct.
</Note>

## Leave or delete an organization

Use the organization access controls to leave an organization or, as its owner, delete it. Review the confirmation before continuing.

Leaving removes your collaboration access. Deleting removes memberships, invitations and organization credentials. The customer's subscription, registered servers and services remain on the owner's account.
