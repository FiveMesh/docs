A workspace is the account or organization context in which you view and operate services.

In V2, the customer account owns billing, registered servers and services. Organizations grant collaboration on their owner's resources. Creating or deleting an organization does not move subscriptions or resources to another owner.

## My account

Choose **My account** in the sidebar selector to operate your own resources:

- Your subscription and usage allowances.
- Your registered cfx.re servers.
- Your CDN workspace.
- Cache, Voice and Logs for your registered servers.
- Your Server API Keys and Developer API Keys.

You do not need an organization to subscribe or configure services.

## Organization context

Choose an organization when you need to collaborate on its owner's services. Your access depends on membership, role, service-sharing settings and server grants.

The owner's subscription supplies the shared service entitlement and quotas. Joining an organization does not move your personal resources into it or combine your subscription with the owner's plan. Switching context does not multiply allowances.

## Switch context

1. Open the server selector at the top of the sidebar.
2. Choose **My account** or an organization you belong to.
3. Choose the server you want to operate.
4. Open **CDN**, **Cache**, **Voice** or **Logs** and check the service state.

CDN stays account-wide. The other three services follow your selected server.

<Warning>
  Check both selections before uploading files, purging Cache, restarting Voice or rotating a Server API Key.
</Warning>

## When access looks wrong

- Confirm the account or organization and server in the selector.
- Check the resource owner's plan and service activation.
- Ask the organization owner to check service sharing, your role and your server access.
- Return to **My account** to see your own resources.

**Billing** always operates on your own customer account, including when you have selected someone else's organization.
