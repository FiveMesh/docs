Billing manages the subscription owned by your customer account. Open **Billing** in the [V2 dashboard](https://app.fivemesh.io/billing).

Active and trialing subscriptions allow service setup according to the plan. An account without a subscription can still sign in and view the dashboard.

CDN, Cache and Voice are included in the self-service plans. Logs requires Growth or Pro. Use the [pricing page](https://fivemesh.io/pricing) and dashboard billing state for current allowances and any custom subscription restrictions.

## What to check

Open **Billing** to review your subscription, renewal dates, invoice history, usage and billing portal actions. Billing always manages your own account, even when you have selected an organization belonging to another customer.

Check billing when:

- A service says an active subscription is required.
- A checkout or plan change just completed.
- You need to review usage against your subscription allowances.
- You need to update payment details or manage renewal in the billing portal.

## Access states

| State | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Active or trialing | Continue to service setup. |
| No active subscription | Pick a plan or complete checkout. |
| Billing needs attention | Update the subscription before access changes. |
| Shared service is locked | Ask the resource owner to check their plan and organization sharing. Your own Billing page cannot change their subscription. |

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Usage and estimates

Usage is aggregated across the customer's services and servers within the subscription billing period. Organization collaboration does not create another allowance. Review requests, bandwidth, storage and Log Events in Billing.

Estimates can lag while meters synchronize. A missing meter is unavailable, not zero. Pace projections need at least one full day of usage and assume the same traffic pace continues. They exclude plan fees and tax; the final invoice comes from the billing provider.

Use **Open billing portal** for available subscription and payment actions. Scheduled cancellation keeps access until the period end shown in the dashboard.

## Pricing currency

Plan prices support the selected subscription currency. All overage rates and
estimates stay in **EUR**, including Logs. A non-EUR plan amount and EUR overages
are shown separately; the final invoice amount comes from Polar.

## Logs usage

**Log Events** counts accepted events across the customer's servers in the subscription billing period. Growth includes **5 million events/month** and Pro includes **25 million events/month**. Starter does not include Logs. Rejected batches and retries of an accepted batch do not add usage.

The planned rate is **€1 per additional million accepted events**. Logs overage is not charged during observation. Any end-of-period projection is an estimate at the planned rate, not an amount due. See [Logs operations](/logs/operations#usage-and-allowances) for counting and retry behavior.
