Billing controls whether the active workspace can use FiveMesh services.

The dashboard treats active and trialing subscriptions as service access. When a workspace does not have active access, CDN, Cache and Voice setup remain locked.

Voice can also require a plan that includes the service. Use the pricing page and dashboard billing state as the source of truth for current plan limits.

## What to check

Open **Billing** from the dashboard sidebar to review the current workspace subscription state.

Check billing when:

- A service says an active subscription is required.
- A checkout or plan change just completed.
- You switched from personal workspace to an organization.
- You expected an organization to share services but the service is still locked.

## Access states

| State | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Active or trialing | Continue to service setup. |
| No active subscription | Pick a plan or complete checkout. |
| Billing needs attention | Update the subscription before access changes. |
| Organization has no shared subscription | Ask the owner to activate billing or switch back to your personal workspace. |

## After checkout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Return to the dashboard">
    After checkout, return to **Billing** or **Overview**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Refresh the workspace state">
    If the dashboard has not updated yet, refresh the page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the service">
    Open **CDN**, **Cache** or **Voice** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pricing currency

Plan prices support the selected subscription currency. All overage rates and
estimates stay in **EUR**, including Logs. A non-EUR plan amount and EUR overages
are shown separately; the final invoice amount comes from Polar.

## Logs usage

**Log Events** counts accepted events across the customer's servers in the subscription billing period. Growth includes **5 million events/month** and Pro includes **25 million events/month**. Starter does not include Logs. Rejected batches and retries of an accepted batch do not add usage.

The planned rate is **€1 per additional million accepted events**. Logs overage is not charged during observation. Any end-of-period projection is an estimate at the planned rate, not an amount due. See [Logs operations](/logs/operations#usage-and-allowances) for counting and retry behavior.
