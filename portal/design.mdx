Open **Portal** → **Design**. The studio has a sidebar with two groups, **Look** and **Content**, and shows one panel at a time. A bar at the top shows whether you have unsaved changes and holds **Discard**, **Preview** and **Publish**.

You never write CSS or HTML. Every option is built to stay readable and safe for your players.

## Look

### Theme

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Preset** | FiveMesh, Noir, Midnight, Ember, Forest, Neon or Sand. |
| **Appearance** | System, Light or Dark. |
| **Accent color** | The theme's accent or your own color. |
| **Buttons** | Ink or accent buttons. |
| **Corners** | Sharp, soft or round. |
| **Heading font** | Sans, serif, mono or rounded. |

### Header

Choose how your homepage opens:

| Layout | Look |
| --- | --- |
| **Standard** | Banner above, name beside the logo. |
| **Cover** | Name over your banner. |
| **Centered** | Everything centered. |
| **Split** | Name beside a large image. |
| **Minimal** | Just your name, no banner. |
| **Full width** | Edge-to-edge banner. |
| **Video** | A muted video loop behind your name. |

Turn **Show banner image** off for a clean header in your accent color. **Height** (compact, medium or tall) applies to the cover, split, full-width and video layouts.

For the **Video** layout, paste an `https://` link to an `.mp4` or `.webm` file, ideally a short muted loop uploaded to your CDN. Your banner image shows while the video loads and for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

The banner image and logo come from **Settings** → community branding.

### Homepage

Choose which sections your homepage shows and in which order: **Forms**, **Media**, **Links**, **Pages**, **FAQ**, **Team** and **Gallery**. Empty sections stay hidden.

### Links and announcement

- **Links**: up to 6 links with a label, for example your store, rules or Discord invite. They must use `https://`.
- **Announcement**: a one-line bar at the top of every page, up to 140 characters, with an optional link.

## Content

Add pages, an FAQ, your staff team and a gallery. See [Pages and homepage content](/portal/pages).

## Preview and publish

- **Preview** opens your Portal with your unsaved changes through a private link that lasts 2 hours. A **Preview** bar with **Exit** shows on every page. Other visitors keep seeing the published version.
- **Publish** saves your design and content together. Changes are live right away.
- **Discard** returns to the last published version.
