Each form has its own notification settings under **Forms** → your form → **Notifications**. There are three kinds.

## Staff notifications

Post a message in one of your Discord channels when something needs your team.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Team channel** | A text or announcement channel in your Discord server. Pick it from the list or enter its ID. |
| **Role to mention** | Optional. This role is pinged with each staff message. Leave it empty to post quietly. |
| **New applications** | Posts when a player submits. |
| **Application updated by the applicant** | Posts when a player updates their answers. |
| **Replies from applicants** | Posts when a player replies. |

The FiveMesh bot needs **View Channel**, **Send Messages** and **Embed Links** in the channel.

## Applicant updates channel

Post updates in a channel your players can read, such as `#applications`, and mention the applicant. This works even when players did not turn on notifications.

Choose a channel, then which events to post: **Application received**, **Review started and decisions**, and **Team replies**.

<Note>
  Decisions are posted without their outcome: the message tells the applicant a decision was made and links to their application. A refusal is never shown publicly.
</Note>

## Applicant notifications

Players choose how they hear about their applications. **Nobody is messaged without opting in.** In the form settings you decide which channels players may choose and which events notify them:

- **Let applicants opt in to Discord DMs**: messages from the FiveMesh bot.
- **Let applicants opt in to email**: emails from `no-reply@fivemesh.io` with your community's name, sent only to addresses the player confirmed with a code.
- Events: **Application received**, **Status changes** and **Replies from the team**.

Players turn these on in their Portal profile. See [Apply as a player](/portal/applying#notifications).

### Delivery

- Messages contain a link to the application, never the answers or private notes.
- Discord DMs are paced so the bot is never flagged as spam. When several updates happen close together, the player gets one message summarizing them.
- If a player's DMs are closed, DMs to them pause for 7 days. The update is always visible on their application page.
- Emails include an **Unsubscribe** link to the player's profile.
