Forms and Design are the newest parts of FiveMesh Portal. They are ready to use, and they are still in **open beta**: things may break, move or change while we improve them.

| Feature | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| **Forms** | Whitelist, staff and job applications on your Portal, reviewed by your team, with Discord and email notifications. See [Application forms](/portal/forms). |
| **Design** | Themes, header layouts, homepage sections, links, an announcement bar and custom pages. See [Customize your Portal](/portal/design). |

## Join the beta

Only the **server owner** can join, because the beta is enabled on the owner's account. Organization members see who to ask instead of a join button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Portal Overview">
    Select your registered server in the dashboard and open **Portal**. The **Overview** tab shows a **Forms & Design** card with an **Open beta** label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the beta">
    Select **Join the beta**. A dialog explains what Forms and Design include and what open beta means.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Check **I understand Forms & Design are in beta and may break or change**, then select **Join the beta**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Forms** and **Design** tabs appear right away, each with a **BETA** badge. Overview and **Analytics** start showing application activity next to uploads.

## What to expect

- Features can change between visits. Your forms, applications, design and pages are kept as they change.
- Some edges are rough. If something does not work, try again later and tell us about it.
- Avoid relying on the beta for anything critical yet, such as your only whitelist process during a launch.

## Send feedback

The **Forms** and **Design** tabs start with a short beta bar. Select **Send feedback** to tell us what broke, what confused you or what you are missing. Every report is read.

You can also use **Send feedback** in the dashboard top bar at any time.
