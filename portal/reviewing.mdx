Your review team works on the Portal itself, at `/your-community/review`. Reviewers sign in with Discord. They see the forms they review and nothing else.

## Who can review

A player can review a form when they have one of the form's review roles in your Discord server, or when their Discord user ID is listed in the form's **Review team**. Set this in the dashboard under **Forms** → your form → **Review team**.

The **Review team** tab also shows the review space link with **Copy link** and **Open**, so you can share it with your staff.

## The review queue

The queue lists applications newest first. Use it to:

- Filter by status: **New**, **In review**, **Accepted** or **Rejected**, with counts.
- Filter by form.
- Search by applicant name or Discord ID.

New applications and applications with new activity are marked until a reviewer opens them.

## Review an application

Open an application to see:

- **Answers**, numbered and grouped by section, with attached files and document links.
- **Activity**, a timeline of status changes, replies and private notes.
- **Versions**, when the applicant updated their answers. Compare versions to see what changed.
- **Decision**, the current status and actions.

### Reply or add a private note

Use the composer under the timeline:

- **Private note**: visible to the review team only. Notes use a dashed style so they stand out.
- **Reply to applicant**: visible to the applicant on their application page.

### Make a decision

Use the **Decision** panel:

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Mark as in review** | Tells the applicant that someone picked up their application. |
| **Accept application** | Accepts it. You can add a message. If the form has a role on acceptance, the bot gives it. |
| **Reject application** | Rejects it. You can describe why. |
| **Move back to new** | Returns it to the queue. |

The applicant sees the new status on their application page and is notified through the channels they chose. See [Notifications](/portal/notifications).

When the bot cannot give the role on acceptance, a private note explains why, for example missing permissions or a player who left the server.

## Insights

The dashboard **Analytics** tab has a **Forms** view with applications and decisions per day, the review backlog, your acceptance rate, the median time to a decision and a breakdown per form.
