This page is for players. Share it with your community, or link it from a Portal page.

## Apply

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Open the community's Portal and select **Forms**, or follow the form link the community shared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Discord">
    Select **Sign in with Discord**. You need to be a member of the community's Discord server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the questions">
    Your answers are saved in your browser as you type, so you can come back later. Questions marked with an asterisk are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Check your answers and select **Submit application**. You can apply once per form.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Attach files and documents

- **File upload** questions list the file types, size limit and number of files they accept. Anyone with a file's link can open it.
- **Document link** questions accept Google Docs or Canva links. Set Google Docs sharing to **Anyone with the link** so the team can read it.

## Follow your application

Open **Forms** → **My applications**, or your Portal **Profile**, and select your application. The page shows:

- Its status: **New**, **In review**, **Accepted** or **Not accepted**.
- The conversation with the team. Reply there if they ask you something.
- Your answers. If the community allows it, select **Update my answers** to change them until a decision is made.

## Notifications

Nothing is sent to you unless you turn it on. Open your Portal **Profile** → **Notifications**:

- **Discord DMs**: turn the switch on to get messages from the FiveMesh bot. Allow DMs from members of the community's server.
- **Email**: turn the switch on, enter your address and select **Send code**. Enter the six-digit code from the email to confirm. Codes expire after 15 minutes.

Your application page shows which channels will notify you. Turn a switch off at any time, or use **Unsubscribe** in any email.

Some communities also post updates in one of their Discord channels and mention you there. Those posts never say whether you were accepted; open your application to see the decision.
